# Layer controls

Layers are listed front to back. Image channels render in reverse list order, then the segmentation layers (also in reverse list order), Xenium cells, Xenium nuclei, the voxel prior, masks, and transcripts. Xenium vectors and the voxel prior are displayed in the 2-D view. The 3-D view retains image channels, cell bodies, and transcripts. While Go deeper's window is open on a cell (2-D), its veil is drawn last, over every 2-D layer (and, while its suspect transcripts glow, a second veil inside the lit cells, under the transcripts or its dots); its suspect transcripts glow on a canvas of their own between the stage and the overlay; the overlay carries its cell's starting outline and a test of a setting's square (`setFocus`, `setGlow`, `setStartOutline`, `setTestSquare` below).

`stage.set({cells, cellColorBy, transcriptColorBy, transcriptSize, transcriptAlpha})` controls cell visibility and transcript appearance in both views. `cellColorBy: 'identity'` and `transcriptColorBy: 'cell'` are the current modes. The `'cluster'` options use the active analysis result, with identity colours until a result is available.

`stage.setImage(rect, channels, keepPrevious, mipmaps)` uploads image data. Each channel has `source` (an image/canvas, `{data: Uint16Array, width, height}`, or null for a channel a whole-sample view has not loaded: only the channels that are on are fetched, and a channel without a source has no texture and is not drawn), `on`, `low`, `high`, `gamma`, `colormap`, `opacity`, and `blending`. With `keepPrevious` (whole-sample views) the textures on screen stay until the replacement is uploaded, and a channel whose source is unchanged over the same rect keeps its texture, so turning a channel on uploads only that channel. In 2-D, view images are sampled LINEAR without a mipmap chain; in 3-D the tilted ground plane shows them up to ~4x smaller, so every image texture gets its chain (`stage.mipmapImage()` on the switch to 3-D, at upload afterwards); the whole-run image of a detail run always has its mipmaps. Whole-sample runs list every channel from the start (`main.js` `loadRunImage`, sources null until loaded), so a channel saved as hidden can always be turned on again. A second lost context within a minute halves `stage.imageLimit` from the largest image side in use (`stage.imageSize`). No GL error query follows an upload: a failed allocation loses the context, and the restored context gets the image and the frame uploaded again. `stage.setChannels(channels)` changes uniforms without uploading source pixels again. The panel mutates these same channel objects and marks the stage dirty. `EXT_texture_norm16` enables filterable R16 textures; missing support/files use PNGs and 0–255 contrast. LUTs are generated from colour ramps and interpolated palette anchors into 256×1 RGB textures.

`PriorLayers` loads run Xenium vectors and the binary voxel prior, projects the strongest prior across z (or uses the chosen z slice), and composites below masks. Vectors are `PolygonSet`s (`polygons.mjs`: flat typed arrays with a bbox grid index built once per set; legacy JSON polygon arrays are converted when assigned). Its screen-resolution vector canvas is drawn again when the data or styling changes, and when the camera or stage size has settled: while the camera moves (wheel, drag, fit tween) or the stage is resized (the UMAP pane), the last canvas is drawn transformed to the moving camera, and one fresh drawing replaces it once the stage has been still for 140 ms, identical to what one drawing at that camera gives. Each outline layer is one stroke of one `Path2D` of the polygons near the view (`prior-paths.mjs`): one path keeps the shared edges of neighbouring cells drawn once, as before, and its polygons are appended per grid cell from one-polygon paths, so it is built in linear time (the old single path called `closePath` on an ever larger path: tens of seconds for a whole sample). Tiles of polygons smaller than a device pixel are stroked as their boxes. Picker vector requests are debounced and ignore stale responses; a pan or zoom keeps the outlines on screen until the new box's arrive, and only the layers chosen as shapes priors are drawn.

Segmentation layers: Layers > Load Other Mask lists any cell-like shapes element of the open sample (each called a mask there), after the run's own outlines (Prior shapes, or a stain run's Cell outlines) and before the image channels. `seg-layers.mjs` `SegLayers` is the store, one sample at a time (`priors.segments.setSample(id)`, from main.js for a zarr sample's run or picker; null otherwise). "Add a mask…" reads the sample's list once (`GET /api/v1/samples/{id}/segmentations`) and offers the elements not listed yet by their names and keys; a layer's outlines are fetched the first time it is shown (`GET .../segmentations/{key}/outlines`, once the list is read its own URL naming the element's version, `?v=`: the prior vectors binary with its cells only, kept by the browser for good at that version, decoded once by `decodeVectors`), never while it is hidden (hiding stops a fetch on its way), and at most `MAX_SHOWN` = 4 show at once: a fifth shown, or one added while four show (it is then listed hidden), says so in the group's note instead (never greyed out). A row is its eye, its colour and its name over its key (or "Loading…" / "Could not load it" with Try again); selected, its fields are Colour, Opacity, Line width, Show as (Outlines, or Filled: the union filled at `FILL_SHARE` = 0.3 of its opacity under its stroke) and Remove. The list per sample and each layer's style per sample and key (kept after Remove) are remembered in `sopa-companion:segmentations:v1` (the 40 samples used last); the last sample's layers are kept aside with their outlines, so the picker between two runs of a sample fetches nothing again. `PriorLayers` draws them on a raster of their own (`SegRaster`: its own canvas, texture, full-stage quad and worker, under the run's own raster, by the same rules: drawn again only when they or the camera change, moved with a moving camera meanwhile), so a change to either never paints or uploads the other and the run's canvas is exactly as before; each layer is one path of its polygons near the view (one union stroke, filled first when Filled), the last listed first (the first on top), always drawn by `prior-raster-worker.mjs` where there is one, on any page (each set cloned to it once while drawn; one no longer drawn is let go there), else on the main thread. 2-D only; in the picker they are drawn by their own eye, not as the prior preview is. `layerPanel.mountSegmentations(host)` draws the same group, without the fields, into another element (the picker's card, where Layers is hidden). The development harness reads `priors.segmentationsShown()` (each loaded layer's key, name, visible, colour, style, state and whether it is drawn on screen now) and the counter `segLayerDraws` (each new raster of them installed; registered at 0). Measured in a headless page (software canvas, 1600 × 900, three layers of 43-45k polygons each): on the main thread a settled view costs 24-34 ms at 1.6 mm across, 44-49 ms at 3.2 mm and ~125 ms for the whole sample (painting plus the upload that rasterises it); through the worker the main thread pays ~2 ms per view (the bitmap's upload) and ~16 ms once to send the three layers, the worker 30, 45-50 and 125-130 ms.

`api.createRun(sample, region, prior, options, source)` starts a run; every run starts paused on its prior and follows proseg's own schedule (there is no checkbox and no schedule choice). Prior visibility follows frame 0 until explicitly toggled, after which the user's choice is retained.

Layer preferences use `sopa-companion:layers:v1` in optional localStorage. An image channel's look (on, gamma, opacity, colour, blending) is kept by channel name; its contrast limits (low, high, depth) per sample under `contrast` (`{<sample id>: {<channel name>: {low, high, depth}}}`, the `CONTRAST_SAMPLES` = 50 samples changed last), so one sample's limits never saturate another's image of the same channel name, and limits kept by name (older versions) are dropped when the preferences are read; contrast resets to the appropriate defaults when switching between uint16 and PNG intensity spaces. The display modes are Cells (`mode` 0, key 1) and Outlines (1, key 2): a saved mode the stage no longer has (the Activity mode's 2, from an older version) is dropped when the preferences are read, so Cells stands, and the preferences are written back once without it (`layer-panel.js` `savedStage`).

Clusters & UMAP are always on (no switch; the engine analyses every active run from its first frame, and the pane opens with the run). `analysis.js` owns cancellable result loading, a bounded typed-array cache, historical requests, legend and the resizable UMAP pane. Historical frames only use results at or before their sequence; frame upload never awaits analysis. Replay requests are coalesced over 150 ms so continuous playback still requests analysis. While following a live run, a downloaded result is applied with the next snapshot (at most 1 s later without one), so they share its paint (what the analysis is doing, each result's step times included, goes to the page log behind the Log button, never on the page); a run of 20,000 cells or more gets a new result at most once a second, also with a snapshot (the timer that ends that window only hands the result to the next snapshot). Every new UMAP layout flies, at any map size (`umap.js` `morphDuration`: 0.9 of the time since the previous layout, 200-900 ms). The pane width is remembered separately as `sopa-companion:umap-width`. The clusters' legend under Layers > Cells holds still while a run plays (`clusters.js` `LEGEND_ROWS`, `ROW_PX`): rows of one fixed height, at most 8 shown (more scroll inside it), and a height that only grows within a run, so nothing below it in Layers moves as clusters come and go. A run the engine cannot cluster (`unavailable`: too few cells in a small region, or an old run without exported transcripts) keeps the pane closed and shows one plain line in the legend's place instead of a failure (`analysis.js` `UNAVAILABLE`, `block`), and an engine without the GPU worker's environment (/health `analysis` false) opens runs without the pane, so it never flashes open and shut.

`stage.setFocus({cell, sources: [[label, share]], same: [labels], hot, lit: [labels], dim} | null)` (Go deeper's window, `qc-deeper.js`) outlines one cell and its neighbours in the 2-D mask pass: the window's cell white, up to `FOCUS_SOURCES` = 5 source neighbours magenta with an opacity by their share of its RNA (35 % at 1 %, full from 40 %), up to `FOCUS_SAME` = 10 same-kind neighbours dotted grey, and `hot` (a row hovered or pinned) 1 px wider. The outlines never touch a fill (the Mixing colours use fills). It also lights `lit` (the cell first, each once, at most `FOCUS_LIT` = 6; by default the cell and its sources) and greys everything else by `dim` (0-1, by default 1): a veil of its own pass (full viewport, drawn last in 2-D: over the image channels, the Xenium masks, the priors canvas, the masks and the transcripts or Go deeper's dots, so piled-up transcripts darken too) lays `VEIL_COLOR` at `FOCUS_VEIL` = 0.62 x the focus's strength outside the lit cells' footprints, read from the frame's label texture, with a soft edge `FOCUS_SOFT_PX` = 2.5 CSS px wide (one texel tap per neighbour voxel within reach); the other proseg cells go `FOCUS_GREY` = 60 % toward grey at `FOCUS_CELL_ALPHA` = 0.22 of their alpha (on `col` after the trails, never the fill), their transcripts to `FOCUS_TX_ALPHA` = 0.15 (a focus variant of the transcripts' program, compiled at init), the arrows unchanged (the overlay has no area outline or chip to shade), and the selection's dim gives way by the same strength. The strength eases in over `FOCUS_IN_MS` = 320 ms (on open and on a new cell, from none) and out over `FOCUS_OUT_MS` = 220 ms (on close), in `FOCUS_FRAMES` = 4 smoothed steps on the stage's own plan (`focusPlan`/`focusValue`/`focusNext`, like the Mixing cross-fade), then nothing is scheduled; at once with reduced or calm motion. `stage.focus` is the focus as set (null at once on close; `lit` a Uint32Array(6)), `stage.focusLevel` the strength drawn now. 3-D: none of it. Everything is gated by `uFocusOn` and the focus programs, so with the window closed (its ease over) the GL calls and uniforms are exactly those of before; it returns whether anything changed (no redraw otherwise). The arrows from the sources to the cell are drawn by `qc-overlay.mjs` `DeeperArrows` on the overlay canvas, and the UMAP rings the same cells with `UmapView.ringCells([[index, kind]] | null)` (`uRingCount` 0: nothing drawn).

`stage.setGlow({key, points: Float32Array [x µm, y µm, p] …, giver: [x, y] µm | null} | null)` (`glow.mjs` `SuspectGlow`) makes Go deeper's suspect transcripts glow: on a 2-D canvas (`#glow`, `aria-hidden`, no pointer events) inserted right after the stage's canvas, so between it and the overlay, sized and placed to the glowing points' box on screen plus the halo (clipped to the stage), and placed again and redrawn in the stage's frame on every camera change. Each point (finite place, p > 0, p over 1 as 1) is a soft `GLOW_COLOR` #ff4fa3 halo (`GLOW_HALO` 6x the window's dot, 30-56 CSS px across, added where they overlap) under a white-pink core (`GLOW_CORE` 0.8x the dot, at least 4 px; `HALO_STOPS`, `CORE_STOPS`), as strong as p, breathing between 60 % and 100 % over 1.6 s (the core between 85 % and 100 %), the breath rippling in from the giver's side (each point's phase by its place along the line from the giver to the points' middle, up to half a breath). Its frames come through the stage's scheduler as an animator (joined only while it has points; `frame()` returns false): at most `GLOW_FPS` = 12 a second on a fixed grid, never a stage draw and never the mixing check's motion, counted as `glowFrames` (registered at 0 when the stage is made; `glow.mjs` `COUNTERS`). Nothing is scheduled while it is off, the page hidden, in 3-D (hidden in the next frame), all its points off screen, or with the context lost (hidden at once); reduced or calm motion: drawn once per change at full strength. The window's dots step back to `GLOW_DOTS` = 0.5 of their opacity while it glows, and while the focus is on the lit cells dim inside their outlines: the veil's program in a pass of its own after the masks and before the transcripts or dots (`uVeil` 0, `uInner` = `FOCUS_INNER` 0.5 x the focus's strength x `stage.innerLevel`), laying `VEIL_COLOR` inside the lit cells except on their outlines, which it finds as the mask does (a label change within the outline's width of the voxel's edge: `edgePx`, a hot source's 1 CSS px wider; none with Cells hidden), so the image and the cells' fill dim there while the outlines, the dots and the glow do not; the outer veil (`uInner` 0) stays last. `stage.innerLevel` eases in over 320 ms when the glow comes and out over 220 ms when it goes (the focus's `focusPlan`, 4 steps), at once with reduced or calm motion, in 3-D or without a focus. The stage redraws once when the glow comes or goes while the dots or the focus are on, never otherwise. The same key, number of points and giver again: no work. `stage.glow` is `{on, count, fps}` for the harness (on: set and on screen; fps: 12 while it breathes, 0 static or off).

`stage.setStartOutline({points: Float32Array of [x, y] µm pairs, closed} | null)` keeps Go deeper's starting outline of its cell while its Show is on (`stage.startOutline` = `{points (a copy), closed, count}` or null; at least two finite points); the stage's own drawing never changes for it (`main.js` passes it, from `PriorLayers.startOutline` below). `qc-overlay.mjs` draws it on the overlay canvas (over the stage, the glow and the mixing check's per-bin layer, under Go deeper's arrows) as a white 1.5 CSS px line in 4/3 px dashes over a dark casing (`START_STYLE`), never shaded by the focus, in a refresh frame the stage's scheduler gives it when it changes (`QcOverlay.marksDue`; not motion). `prior-layers.js` `PriorLayers.startOutline(name, label)` gives the polygon: the prior's own polygon with the cell's name (the vectors' `polygonIds`, or numeric ids; the largest part of a name with several), else the outline of the cell's label in the prior's labels (`labelOutline`: the outer boundary of its largest 4-connected part along the voxel edges, corners only; a detail run's whole prior projected once, a whole-sample run's view raster), else null; the answer is kept until the vectors or the prior change.

`QcOverlay.setTestSquare({box: [x0, y0, x1, y1] µm} | null)` draws a test of a setting's square while the test exists: a light 1.5 CSS px line in 7/5 px dashes (`DASHES`) over the dark casing (`SQUARE_STYLE`), at full strength, in a refresh frame like the starting outline's; both marks are drawn whether or not the mixing check's own layer is (`visible`), never in 3-D. `QcOverlay.testSquare` is the box set. `MixingLegend.dock(corner, height)` moves the Mixing legend above the test corner while the corner (its box as `TestCorner` reports it) lies where the legend sits (`LEGEND_PLACE`; `height` the legend's own, at most 104 px), and back to its place without one; it returns the legend's bottom (CSS px).

`stage.setAnalysis(result)` uploads the 4096-wide R16I label-to-cluster texture only when the displayed result changes. Label zero is background, and cell index i maps to label i+1. A separate RGBA cluster-style texture maps stable cluster IDs to palette colours and visibility: a summary's `color` is the palette index the GPU worker chose for that cluster (`analysis/worker.py` `assign_colours`), so no two clusters on screen share a colour. Legend hover uses a uniform; hiding clusters changes only the style texture. Cells and transcripts can independently select `cluster` or their identity mode; the first result ever shown in a browser selects cluster for both, once (`sopa-companion:cluster-colours:v1`; Mixing colours are never replaced), and from then on the layer panel's saved choice wins, so Identity sticks. A tool switching a run's analysis off restores identity rendering without discarding those mode preferences. UMAP IDs remain zero-based and are translated at the tissue boundary.

Xenium cell/nucleus masks start disabled on each run and load together on first enable. `xenium-masks.js` retains the original labels for hover and boundary detection, uploads nearest-sampled R32UI textures within GPU limits, and maps labels through Xenium IDs to the same identity/cluster colours as proseg. Dropped cells are grey. These layers render below the prior layers in 2-D, with independent opacity and outline-only controls.

In 2-D, transcripts use the cell colour mode and their own 3-D voxel owner. The consensus texture is retained separately from the chosen z slice; assigned points outside their consensus footprint have an optional thin ring. Points composite last. Size uses a logarithmic 0.5–12 px slider and `[` / `]` shortcuts in both views.

`cellstats.mjs` maintains a six-frame, cancellable statistics cache independent of clustering: a snapshot's statistics are fetched alongside its download (`prefetch`, leaving `current` alone) and become current when it is shown (`load`), so readouts of the frame on screen never blank while the next one downloads. Hover, cell cards, UMAP centring and the top-eight activity list use the frame on screen. Cell cards fetch the selected cell's whole-run history (zero-based cell index), plotting area and transcripts against iteration with the current iteration marked. No cell readout scans frame rasters.
